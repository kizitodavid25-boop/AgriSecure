Report a Food Crisis

If your community is facing food insecurity, drought, flooding, pest outbreak, or supply disruption — report it here to trigger aid response.

Submit Crisis Report

All reports are reviewed within 12 hours by our crisis coordination team.

What Happens Next?

1

Report ReceivedSubmitted to our crisis coordination dashboard within seconds.

2

VerificationA local field coordinator contacts you within 12 hours to verify details.

3

Alert IssuedAn alert is sent to NGO partners, government agencies, and aid organizations.

4

Aid DispatchedEmergency seeds, food, or technical support is mobilized within 2–5 days.
