About AgroSecure

Rooted in Uganda, growing toward a food-secure world — understand our mission, our approach, and the SDG that drives us.

Our Mission

Ending Hunger Through Digital Empowerment

AgroSecure is a web-based agricultural support platform designed to address the food insecurity crisis affecting millions of Ugandans. We connect smallholder farmers with the tools, information, and networks they need to grow more, waste less, and build resilience.

Aligned with SDG 2 – Zero Hunger, our platform provides real-time crop guidance, crisis reporting tools, nutritional resources, and market linkages — all accessible on mobile and desktop.

We believe technology can close the gap between farmers in rural Uganda and the knowledge they deserve. Every harvest saved, every crisis reported, every connection made brings us closer to food security for all.

The Goal

Understanding SDG 2: Zero Hunger

SDG 2 is one of 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals — a global blueprint to achieve a better, more sustainable future by 2030.

2.1

End Hunger

Ensure access to safe, nutritious, and sufficient food for all people, especially the poor and vulnerable including infants.

2.2

End Malnutrition

End all forms of malnutrition, including achieving targets on stunting and wasting in children under 5 years of age.

2.3

Double Productivity

Double the agricultural productivity and incomes of small-scale food producers — especially women, indigenous peoples, and family farmers.

2.4

Sustainable Agriculture

Ensure sustainable food production systems and implement resilient practices that increase productivity and production.

2.5

Genetic Diversity

Maintain the genetic diversity of seeds, cultivated plants, farmed and domesticated animals and their related wild species.

2030

Target Year

All SDG 2 targets must be achieved by 2030 — time is running out and digital solutions like AgroSecure are critical.

The Team

Built by Students, For Communities

AgroSecure was created by a student group at Makerere University Business School (MUBS) as part of an ICT project aimed at applying technology for social good.

Our team comprises web developers, data analysts, and agricultural enthusiasts who conducted field research in Wakiso, Gulu, and Mbarara districts before building this platform.

We are guided by the belief that technology is most powerful when it solves real problems for real people — not just showcases innovation.
