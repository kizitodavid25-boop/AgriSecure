Sustainable Practices

Top Farming Tips for 2026

Intercropping

Plant maize and beans together. Beans fix nitrogen in the soil, reducing fertilizer costs by up to 40% while maintaining high yields.

Composting

Convert crop residues and kitchen waste into compost. Applying 2–4 tonnes of compost per hectare improves soil organic matter significantly.

Water Harvesting

Construct simple zai pits or half-moon catchments to collect rainwater and reduce irrigation needs by up to 50% in dry seasons.

Climate-Smart Varieties

Use drought-tolerant seed varieties like NASE 19 cassava and NARO drought-tolerant maize — proven to yield well under stress conditions.

Post-Harvest Storage

Use hermetic storage bags to reduce post-harvest losses from 30–40% to less than 1%. Protect grain from weevils and moisture.

Farmer Groups

Join or form a farmer cooperative to access bulk input buying, shared machinery, collective bargaining, and group extension services.
